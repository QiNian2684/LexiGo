The original English and Mandarin sentence numbers and usernames are
embedded in the index and shown on the corresponding flashcard.
The supplementary example-pairs index uses the English and Mandarin
detailed sentence files and direct eng-cmn links from the 2026-11-14
weekly export at https://downloads.tatoeba.org/exports/per_language/.
The scripts/build_example_pairs.py script selects the bilingual examples.

Example sentences from Simple English Wiktionary contributors
Source: https://simple.wiktionary.org/
Data extraction: https://kaikki.org/simplewiktionary/rawdata.html
License: CC BY-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The associated headword page and its history identify contributors.
Example text is selected and whitespace can be normalized.
The derived Chinese translations in assets/example-translations.json.gz
are machine-translated or reviewed adaptations of these English examples
and are available under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license. LexiGo marks this
supplement as machine translation and links to the original material.
Build-time translation model: Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-zh, revision
408d9bc410a388e1d9aef112a2daba955b945255.
Model source: https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-zh
Model license: Apache-2.0.
Independent build-time comparison: Meta M2M100 418M (MIT),
https://huggingface.co/facebook/m2m100_418M, from the int8 conversion at
https://huggingface.co/gn64/M2M100_418M_CTranslate2/tree/645286f.
Neither model is included with LexiGo.
Generation and quality filters: scripts/build_example_translations.py.
